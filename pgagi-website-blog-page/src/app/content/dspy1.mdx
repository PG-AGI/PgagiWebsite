## What is DSPy?

A framework for algorithmically optimizing Language Model(LM) prompts and weights.

In simple words, DSPy thinks on our behalf to optimize prompts which saves our time spent on thinking and trying different performing prompt engineering or prompt tuning techniques.

DSPy can teach powerful models like  `GPT-3.5`  or  `GPT-4`  and local models like  `T5-base`  or  `Llama2-13b`  to be much more reliable at tasks.

DSPy is so easy to use as it is inspired by a popular deep learning framework  [PyTorch](https://pytorch.org/).

## What are the things that DSPy provides that can be used to optimize prompts?

-   Signature
-   Modules
-   Optimizers
-   Datasets(limited)
-   Evaluators

**Signature:** Is the most basic form of task description.

**Modules:** Provides the right general-purpose modules, which replace string-based prompting tricks.

**Optimizers:**  Provides us general optimizers, which are algorithms that update parameters in your program.
